Support & Mentoring

Support Beyond the Scholarship

FIRSI's support goes beyond financing. Every Scholar receives comprehensive support throughout their journey.

Soft-Skills Workshops

Public speaking, leadership, stress management, teamwork — cross-cutting skills that make a difference at top schools and in the job market.

Career Preparation

Resume coaching, mock interviews, and direct introductions to partner companies for internships and first jobs.

Settling in Abroad

Welcome guide, housing assistance, help opening a bank account, health insurance — and introductions to FIRSI Scholars already in your city.

Administrative Support

A dedicated advisor for every Scholar — handling renewal files, tracking disbursements, and support with visa and residence permit procedures.

Our Initiatives

Beyond Support

Inspiring Journeys

Meetings with alumni, researchers, and public figures who share their journeys to inspire our Scholars and broaden their sense of what's possible.
