I'm a Company / Partner

Join FIRSI's partner network and gain access to Morocco's top talent while contributing to Africa's development.

Why Join Us

A Strategic Partnership

By partnering with FIRSI, your company gains access to an exceptional talent pool while supporting Africa's development.

Access to Talent

Connect with 3,752 outstanding students and alumni trained at the world's top schools — rare, highly qualified profiles.

Brand & CSR

Associate your brand with a foundation recognized for excellence and social impact. A strong commitment to education and equity.

African Foothold

FIRSI is the bridge between African talent and global companies. Grow your presence on the continent through our network.

Partnership Options

How to Get Involved

Financial Sponsorship

Create a named scholarship in your name or your company's name. Your logo appears on FIRSI's communication materials.

  • Annual scholarship starting at €10,000
  • 3-year named scholarship: €50,000
  • Tax receipt (per Moroccan regulations)

Skills-Based Sponsorship

Make your experts available for workshops, talks, or coaching sessions for our Scholars.

  • Industry and career workshops
  • Mock interviews
  • Company visits

Recruitment Partnership

Get priority access to our Scholars' and alumni's profiles for your internship and job openings.

  • Exclusive job postings
  • Participation in FIRSI career fairs
  • Access to the alumni directory

Academic Partnership

Joint program development, endowed teaching chairs, and research projects with UM6P.

  • Industry chairs
  • Applied research projects
  • Long-term internships
Discuss a Partnership

Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation — affiliated with Mohammed VI Polytechnic University in Benguerir.
