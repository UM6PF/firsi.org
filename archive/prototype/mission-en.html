Mission

Our Mission

Identifying, mentoring, and supporting talented young Moroccans on their path to the world's leading scientific institutions, in Morocco and abroad.

Who We Are

A Foundation Dedicated to Excellence

The Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation (FIRSI) is a Moroccan nonprofit organization affiliated with Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P) in Benguerir. Its mission is to support UM6P's strategic development by mobilizing diverse sources of funding, acting as a structuring catalyst for mentoring high-achieving students, and serving as an incubator for innovative models in education, entrepreneurship, and innovation.

FIRSI is committed to advancing equal opportunity, inclusion, and social diversity — values deeply embedded in UM6P's identity. True to the spirit of Ibn Rochd (Averroes), philosopher and champion of reason and enlightenment, the Foundation believes in the power of knowledge as a lever for individual and collective transformation.

Vision

To position Morocco and Africa as a global hub of scientific excellence, by training a new generation of leaders equipped to address the continent's greatest challenges.

Values

Excellence, equity, inclusion, and social diversity. FIRSI believes that talent knows no social boundaries, and is committed to removing financial barriers so every student can realize their full potential.

Impact 2025–2026

4,546 Scholars supported, 59% women, 48% receiving full funding, 4,154 in Morocco and 392 abroad, across 12 partner institutions.
