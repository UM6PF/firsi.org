Why Support Us

Your Support, A Real Impact

A FIRSI scholarship represents €15,000 to €25,000 a year, allowing a brilliant student without resources to join Polytechnique, CentraleSupélec, or UM6P.

It's an investment with a strong social return: our alumni become engineers, researchers, and entrepreneurs, contributing directly to the economic development of Morocco and Africa.

The Impact of Your Donation

€500Covers one month of a Scholar's living allowance in France
€2,000Funds a Scholar's annual round-trip flight
€10,000Covers a full year of scholarship for one student
€50,000Creates a named scholarship over 3 years for one student
