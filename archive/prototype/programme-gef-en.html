GEF — French Engineering & Business Schools

French Engineering & Business Schools

FIRSI supports top CPGE graduates from Morocco on their way to France's leading engineering schools — with full financial support and personalized mentoring throughout their studies.

Apply View Partner Schools

The Program

FIRSI's Flagship Program

Created to remove the financial barriers keeping Morocco's top students from France's leading engineering schools, GEF is FIRSI's founding program. It is open to CPGE graduates — from LM6E, Moroccan public CPGE programs, or select French CPGE programs — admitted by competitive examination to one of 19 partner engineering schools.

Beyond funding, FIRSI supports every GEF Scholar throughout their studies: soft-skills workshops, career preparation, administrative support, and help settling in abroad — so students can focus fully on their studies.

Who Can Apply

CPGE graduates from LM6E, Moroccan public CPGE programs, or select French CPGE programs (Chimie ParisTech, AgroParisTech, and ESPCI). Admitted by competitive examination into the first year of a Grande École program.

What the Scholarship Covers

Tuition (up to MAD 30,000), living expenses (MAD 60,000), settling-in costs (MAD 6,000), and round-trip France-Morocco airfare (MAD 4,000). Paid in three installments over three years.

The Program in Numbers

392 active GEF Scholars in 2025-2026, 90% of them from LM6E. 1,533 alumni in the FIRSI network. 19 eligible engineering schools.

Eligible Schools
