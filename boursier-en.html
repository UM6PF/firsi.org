Welcome to Your Scholar Portal

You're part of the FIRSI community. Find everything you need here to move forward with confidence in your journey.

Your Journey

FIRSI Supports You Every Step of the Way

Receiving a scholarship is only the beginning. It's a two-way commitment: FIRSI provides comprehensive support, and expects your full engagement in the life of the community in return.

What FIRSI Provides

Financial support
Soft-skills & career workshops
Support settling in abroad
Dedicated administrative support
Alumni community

What We Expect From You

Actively participate in community activities (workshops, events, meetings with alumni)
Meet administrative deadlines (supporting documents, renewal files)
Represent the foundation with integrity at your school and abroad

Your Path

A Three-Stage Journey

Year 1 — Onboarding

Welcome into the FIRSI community, a dedicated point of contact, and initial support workshops.

Year 2 — Consolidation

Enhanced support, participation in community activities, and access to opportunities (mobility, internships depending on program).

Year 3 — Transition

Preparation for entering the workforce, joining the alumni network, and a review of your journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good to Know

How does my scholarship get paid out?

Payment terms depend on your program. Your advisor will explain the details when you join.

What documents do I need to provide, and how often?

A renewal file is required every year. Your advisor will let you know exactly what's needed.

Who should I contact if I run into difficulties?

Academic, financial, or personal — your advisor is your first point of contact, whatever the issue.

What happens if I change schools or programs?

Contact your advisor before taking any steps: a change may affect the terms of your scholarship.

Have another question? Contact your advisor
