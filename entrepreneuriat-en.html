Entrepreneurship & Innovation

Training Talent, Building Companies

Beyond scholarships, FIRSI supports entrepreneurship, regional development, and educational innovation through three complementary initiatives rooted in the UM6P ecosystem.

Entrepreneurship

The Forge

The Forge is FIRSI's entrepreneurship support program, developed in partnership with UM6P Ventures. It supports startup founders from ideation through scale-up, across four progressive phases: pre-incubation, incubation, pre-acceleration, and acceleration.

In 2025, 28 entrepreneurs were selected from 99 applicants, across sectors as varied as Fintech, Agritech, Healthtech, and Cleantech. The Forge is part of the UM6P Ventures and StartGate Rabat ecosystem.

Regional Development

Massarates Rhamna

Launched in 2023 in collaboration with UM6P, the Rhamna Province, and the INDH, Massarates supports the region's population across three areas: skills development, entrepreneurship, and support for cooperatives.

In 2025, 167 beneficiaries were supported — 60% of them women — driving 34 entrepreneurial projects and 21 supported cooperatives, in partnership with the INDH and the OFPPT.

Hybrid Education

HEWB

HEWB (Higher Education Without Borders) is an organization dedicated to expanding access to quality higher education through a hybrid model combining flexible learning, live sessions with experts, and applied training in digital careers. FIRSI is its founding financial partner.

Its flagship program, the Applied Computer Science Program (ACSP), prepares learners for careers in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Digital Factory — with no minimum GPA requirement, hands-on mentorship, and training grounded in real market needs.
