FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Application

Who can apply for FIRSI scholarships?
FIRSI scholarships are open to Moroccan students and, for the UM6P program only, to sub-Saharan African students, subject to financial need and excellent academic results. Each program has its own criteria. See each program's page for specific conditions.
How does the selection process work?
Selection happens in several steps: online application submission, review by the Selection Committee, individual interviews for shortlisted candidates, then notification of the final decision.
When do application campaigns open?
Campaigns open once a year, generally between November and March depending on the program. Follow our news to be informed as soon as they open.
Can I apply to multiple programs at the same time?
No, you can only apply to one program at a time. Choose the program that best matches your academic and career plans.

Scholarships

What exactly does the scholarship cover?
Depending on the program, the scholarship may cover: tuition, a monthly living allowance, and travel costs (round-trip airfare). Details are provided on each program's page.
Is the scholarship renewed automatically?
No. Renewal requires a renewal file every year, including your transcript. Minimum academic requirements must be met, and the renewal campaign deadlines must be respected.
Can I work while holding my scholarship?
Yes, as long as it doesn't affect your academic results. FIRSI even encourages professional experience (internships, assignments) that enriches your journey.

GEF Scholarship — Specific Conditions

I'm in a preparatory class (CPGE). Can I apply for the GEF scholarship?
The GEF scholarship is open to students from Moroccan public CPGE programs and the CPGE program at the Mohammed VI Excellence High School in Benguerir. Students from French CPGE programs may also apply, provided they are admitted to Chimie ParisTech, AgroParisTech, or ESPCI. Moroccan private CPGE programs are not eligible.
I'm a 4th-year student at my school. Can I receive the GEF scholarship?
No. The GEF scholarship is granted for a maximum of three years of study. A student entering directly into a 4th year is therefore not eligible.
I'm in my 2nd year of CPGE and admitted to a non-eligible school. Can I receive the scholarship?
No. FIRSI does not offer scholarships for programs at non-eligible institutions. We encourage you to check the list of eligible schools before submitting your application.
I'm in my 1st year of GEM and want to retake the French entrance exams as an independent candidate. If I get into an eligible school, can I receive the scholarship?
No. The GEF scholarship is intended for CPGE graduates admitted by competitive examination into the first year of an eligible school.
Can I keep my FIRSI scholarship as part of a dual-degree program?
Yes. The scholarship is maintained for up to three years of study, subject to your program's specific conditions, provided you remain enrolled at your home school.
In what cases can the FIRSI scholarship be suspended or discontinued?
The scholarship may be suspended or canceled in the following cases: grade repetition, gap year, apprenticeship or work-study arrangements, a paid internship of more than six months, dropping out of studies, an academic decision by the institution, or any other situation covered by the commitment letter signed by the Scholar. Any interruption of studies must be reported to the Foundation immediately.
I already receive another scholarship covering my tuition. Can I also receive the FIRSI scholarship?
Combining scholarships is reviewed on a case-by-case basis, depending on the program's rules and the nature of the funding already received.
If I change schools or programs after being awarded the scholarship, can I keep it?
Any change must be reported to the Foundation. Continuing the scholarship is subject to program approval and meeting eligibility criteria.
Will I have to repay the FIRSI scholarship after I finish my studies?
No. The FIRSI scholarship is non-repayable financial aid intended for students who lack the resources to finance their studies.
Does the FIRSI scholarship guarantee I'll get a student visa for France?
No. The scholarship is financial support only; obtaining a visa is solely the responsibility of the French consular authorities. Recipients must meet all applicable administrative requirements.

Support & Mentoring

What support does FIRSI provide beyond the financial scholarship?
FIRSI offers comprehensive support: soft-skills workshops, career preparation workshops (resumes, interviews), help settling into the host country, and access to a network of alumni and partner companies.
How do I join the alumni network?
At the end of your program, you'll automatically be invited to join the FIRSI alumni community. You'll then be able to access networking events, job postings, and become a mentor for future Scholars.

The Foundation

Can FIRSI receive donations?
Yes. FIRSI can receive donations and contributions to support its public-interest mission, in compliance with applicable regulations and its internal procedures. Individuals or organizations wishing to contribute can contact the Foundation for guidance.
How does FIRSI protect personal data?
FIRSI processes personal data in accordance with applicable regulations and only for the purposes for which it was collected. Appropriate technical and organizational measures are in place to ensure its security, confidentiality, and integrity.

Contact

How do I submit a complaint or request information from the Foundation?
You can send your request directly by email: equipebourses@firsi.org for any question about your scholarship or application, or contact@firsi.org for any other request. Your message will be handled as quickly as possible.

Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation — affiliated with Mohammed VI Polytechnic University in Benguerir.
