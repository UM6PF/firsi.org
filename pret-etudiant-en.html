Student Loan

The FIRSI Student Loan

A financing program designed so UM6P students can focus fully on their studies, without financial constraints holding back their ambition.

The Program

Support Beyond the Scholarship

Launched in 2023, the FIRSI Student Loan is available to UM6P students whose financial situation calls for additional support to cover tuition and living expenses. It supplements the scholarship for those who already receive one, or serves as an alternative for those who are not eligible.

Its key advantage: FIRSI covers all interest in full. Students repay only the principal borrowed, after completing their studies — with no payments due during their studies or in the year following graduation.

Who Can Benefit

All students enrolled in UM6P's degree programs, whether or not they hold a scholarship, who face an additional financing need for their tuition and campus living expenses.

An Interest-Free Loan

FIRSI covers all interest in full. No repayment during studies or in the year following graduation. Principal repaid over a maximum of 10 years.

A Secure Process

The program relies on a secure process: funds are paid directly to UM6P for tuition and loaded onto a student card in installments for living expenses. Money never passes through the student's personal bank account.
