UM6P

UM6P

FIRSI supports students admitted to Mohammed VI Polytechnic University — a pan-African institution with a global outlook, focused on applied research, innovation, and entrepreneurship in service of Morocco's and Africa's development.

Apply Discover UM6P

The Program

A Platform for Experimentation at the Heart of Africa

UM6P is an institution focused on applied research and innovation. Turned toward Africa, it aims to rank among the world's leading universities in these fields. Its founding campus, located in the town of Benguerir, sits at the heart of Mohammed VI Green City.

More than a traditional academic institution, UM6P is a platform for experimentation and a pool of opportunity — the school of life — committed to an innovative teaching approach that puts hands-on, experiential learning at the heart of its curriculum. UM6P offers a broad range of programs — undergraduate and graduate — built to international standards and backed by cutting-edge research.

A Pan-African University

Founded to contribute to the pan-African journey, UM6P offers a place of emulation run by Africans, for Africans invested in their continent's future.

Research & Innovation

By 2030, UM6P aims to contribute to Morocco's and Africa's economic, social, and environmental development, with research geared toward concrete breakthroughs and Africa's leading start-up campus.

What the FIRSI Scholarship Covers

The FIRSI Scholarship covers tuition as well as living expenses on the Benguerir campus — housing, dining, and access to facilities — so students can fully focus on their studies.
