<p><strong>The French Grandes Écoles Scholarship (GEF)</strong> is offered to outstanding Moroccan students, subject to financial need, who have completed the preparatory classes for the Grandes Écoles (CPGE) and been admitted through competitive entrance exams to the first year of the <strong>“Grande École Program” </strong>at one of the eligible engineering schools. </p><p><strong>Scholarship duration:</strong> The Foundation's scholarship is awarded <strong>for three years of study</strong> starting from the 2024-2025 academic year.</p><p>It is suspended in the event of repeating a year, a gap year, a paid internship, an apprenticeship, a professional contract or discontinuation of studies. </p><p><a href="/assets/docs/ancien-site/2024-04-communiquegef-09042024-2.pdf"><strong>Read the press release here</strong></a></p><p>Application period: MARCH 29 TO MAY 31, 2024</p><p><strong>Follow these instructions to register successfully on our platform:</strong></p><p><strong>Step 1:</strong></p><p>- Fill in and save your form at<strong><a href="https://rb.gy/3l7amh" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> https://rb.gy/3l7amh</a></strong></p><p><strong>Step 2: To get your password </strong></p><p>- Go to the <a href="https://um6p-firsi.eudonet.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Student Area (eudonet.com)</a></p><p>- Click the <strong>“Connexion”</strong> (Log in) button at the top right</p><p>- Click <strong>“première connexion / mot de passe oublié”</strong> (first login / forgot password)</p><p>- Enter your email address.
