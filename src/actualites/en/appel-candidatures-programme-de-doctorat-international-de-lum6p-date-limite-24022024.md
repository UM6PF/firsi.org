<p>The Center for Doctoral Studies (CEDoc) is launching the second cohort of the UM6P<br>International PhD Program.<br>The program offers mentoring, scientific support and scholarships to talented students<br>interested in an academic career.<br>In an interdisciplinary environment, PhD students develop interdisciplinary thinking,<br>skills and experience at the highest level in<br>research-oriented disciplines.<br>The program aims to facilitate the recruitment and foster the success of Moroccan students<br>throughout their doctoral studies, and serves as a lever to help them stand out<br>in their fields of specialization.</p><p><strong>Apply before February 29, 2024: <a href="https://lnkd.in/eeCkQxtC" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://lnkd.in/eeCkQxtC</a></strong></p><p>For more information, contact: <a href="mailto:cedoc@um6p.ma">cedoc@um6p.ma</a></p>
