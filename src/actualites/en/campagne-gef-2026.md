<p>As part of its commitment to excellence, merit and equal opportunity, the Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation - UM6P is launching the French Grandes Écoles Scholarship (GEF) campaign for the 2026–2027 academic year. </p><p>The GEF scholarship is open to graduates of the preparatory classes for the Grandes Écoles (CPGE) who have been admitted through competitive entrance exams to the first year of the Grande École Program at an eligible French engineering school.</p><p><strong>Scholarship duration</strong>: The GEF Scholarship is awarded for three (3) years of study starting from the 2026-2027 academic year. </p><p>It is suspended in the event of repeating a year, a gap year, a long-term paid internship, an apprenticeship, a professional contract or discontinuation of studies.</p><p><strong>Application period</strong>: January 14 to May 15, 2026 </p><p><em><strong>➔ </strong></em><a href="https://www.canva.com/design/DAG9uR79Ygg/hkphIfd9iHibsPytcplNZQ/view?utm_content=DAG9uR79Ygg&amp;utm_campaign=designshare&amp;utm_medium=link2&amp;utm_source=uniquelinks&amp;utlId=ha2e5f21b7f" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><em><strong>Read the 2026-2027 GEF Scholarship Programs press release</strong></em></a></p><p> <a href="https://firsi-um6p.eudonet.com/specif/eudo_07846/Formulaires/#/bourse" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> Apply here </a></p>
