<p>This joint initiative between UM6P, Rhamna Province, the Ibn Rochd Foundation (FIRSI) and the National Initiative for Human Development (INDH) reflects a shared commitment to fostering innovation and supporting high-impact projects. Massarates Rhamna offers three tailored tracks to meet the specific needs of young people in the region: skills development, entrepreneurship support and support for setting up cooperatives.</p><p>The signing ceremony was attended by representatives of UM6P and guests, including the Governor of Rhamna Province, the Secretary General of FIRSI and representatives of the INDH. </p>
