<p>Every year, many talented students with limited financial resources seek to benefit from this aid to pursue higher education. Through its commitment, FIRSI plays a decisive role in ensuring that these deserving young people can access an education of excellence.</p><p>The FIRSI scholarships team provides personalized support to CPGE-bound high school students throughout the application process. This includes organizing information sessions where students and their families can get answers to their questions and understand the eligibility criteria. Hands-on workshops are also held to help students gather all the required documents and complete their applications correctly. This assistance is especially valuable for those with no prior experience of this kind of administrative procedure.</p><p>Thanks to the collaboration between the teams of the Mohammed VI Excellence High Schools (LM6E) and FIRSI, many high school students admitted to the preparatory classes for the Grandes Écoles can now look ahead to their academic future with confidence.</p>
