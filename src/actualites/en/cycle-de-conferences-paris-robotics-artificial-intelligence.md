<p>Rachid Alami, Research Director at LAAS-CNRS, came to share his journey as a researcher in robotics, and more specifically in human-robot interaction.</p><p>This event is part of the Conference Series launched by FIRSI in collaboration with UM6P France to support its Moroccan scholars throughout their academic journey.</p><p>He pointed out that a researcher's job involves many responsibilities, including seeking partnerships or funding and mentoring young people, whether within a research institute or as a faculty researcher.</p><p>Today, robotics is everywhere. It is no longer limited to performing repetitive tasks; it has embraced artificial intelligence. This is a form of robotics capable of interacting finely with its environment based on signals and learning.</p><p>Research aims to develop sensory functions that enable an artificial object to engage physically in its tasks. The applications are many: recognizing human behavior, assisting drivers, or restoring motor functions to people with disabilities.</p><p>Decision-making autonomy is the ultimate goal of work in this field.</p>
