<p>Joël Couse, Special Advisor Directorate of Energy Markets &amp; Security at IEA, shared with students the context of a realistic energy transition that will simultaneously meet economic and environmental challenges.</p><p>This event is part of the conference series launched by FIRSI in collaboration with OCP Paris to support its Moroccan scholars throughout their academic journey.</p><p>On this occasion, Joel R. Couse discussed the energy issue, which has been a concern for some thirty years but now requires nations to carry out a rapid energy transition.</p><p>There is a consensus on the importance of reducing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, but alternative energy sources cannot meet total consumption, and emerging countries are not ready to sacrifice their development to offset pollution emitted mainly by already developed countries.</p><p>Everyone agrees on the need to consume less and better.</p>
