<p>The <strong>IRIS </strong>conference is a scientific and strategic event led by Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), the Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation (FIRSI) and its partners, aiming to explore the role of <strong>innovation and applied research</strong> in Africa's development.</p><p>The clearly defined objectives are as follows: </p><ul><li>Showcase <strong>scientific and technological advances</strong> stemming from academic and industrial research.</li><li>Foster <strong>collaboration among researchers, institutions, startups and decision-makers</strong> from the public and private sectors.</li><li>Discuss <strong>major development challenges</strong> for Africa (health, agriculture, climate, education, energy…) through the lens of science and innovation.</li><li>Encourage the <strong>mobilization of African talent</strong>, particularly from the diaspora.</li></ul><p><em><strong>Quick and easy registration via the QR code</strong></em></p>
