<p>Najlaa Yassine, International Deal Negotiator at TotalEnergies, came to share her expertise on how a negotiation unfolds and how to apply decision-making tools in a volatile environment.</p><p>This event is part of the Conference Series launched by FIRSI in collaboration with UM6P France to support its Moroccan scholars throughout their academic journey.</p><p>Najlaa YASSINE described the mechanisms for conducting an effective negotiation in the commodities sector.</p><p>Such a negotiation is based on a prior macroeconomic analysis of the sector, its rate of return and the potential consequences of a no-deal.</p><p>The approach relies on the principle of the Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement (BATNA), which involves considering the alternatives to the deal in order to prepare an exit from the negotiation. Fallback options put the negotiator in a position of advantage.</p>
