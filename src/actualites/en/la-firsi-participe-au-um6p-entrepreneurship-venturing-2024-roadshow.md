<p><br><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/yassine-laghzioui/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Yassine LAGHZIOUI</a>, CEO of UM6P Ventures, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/tarik-el-fehri-09aa644/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tarik El Fehri</a>, Secretary General of FIRSI, shared their visions of technological innovation and entrepreneurship in Morocco and Africa. <br><br>UM6P Ventures, the investment arm of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University, actively supports startups through its Digital Transformation Startup and Deeptech Ventures funds. Their mission? To promote entrepreneurship and accelerate scientific innovation across the continent. <br><br>FIRSI is working to make Africa a land of advancement and innovation by supporting young people, the scientific community and local regions for sustainable development. <br><br><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/fdidoo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fahd Benkirane</a>, Managing Director of UM6P France, reiterated UM6P France's mission to serve as a bridge between Africa and Europe for knowledge sharing and innovation. <br><br>The discussions highlighted opportunities for project leaders and the importance of building a support ecosystem around research and innovation. Many students attended this inspiring and insightful event.</p>
