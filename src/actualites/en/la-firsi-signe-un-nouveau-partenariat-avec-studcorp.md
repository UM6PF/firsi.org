<p>The Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation (FIRSI) and STUDCORP have established a partnership to support FIRSI scholars in France. </p><p>This partnership aims to offer you <strong>negotiated rates</strong> on:</p><ul><li><em><strong>Health and mobility coverage</strong></em></li><li><em><strong>Home insurance</strong></em></li><li><em><strong>Protection for your digital devices</strong></em></li><li><em><strong>Other benefits </strong></em></li></ul><p>When you arrive in a new country, particularly France, you face a number of challenges, including health coverage. Before you can benefit from the French Social Security system, it is essential to take out private health insurance. </p><p>This is where STUDCORP comes in, offering health coverage tailored to your needs at negotiated rates thanks to this new partnership. </p><p>In addition to health coverage, home insurance is also mandatory in France. </p><p>Finding housing and taking out insurance can be complicated for an international student. That is why STUDCORP makes things easier by offering solutions suited to your situation and budget. </p><p>Finally, this partnership also includes protection for digital devices, which is essential in today's connected world. Studying abroad often means using a laptop, tablet or smartphone to take notes, do research and stay in touch with family and friends. </p><p>This partnership reaffirms FIRSI's commitment to supporting its scholars and helping them settle in smoothly abroad. </p><p><strong><em>For more information: </em><a href="https://studcorp.com/firsi/"><em>https://studcorp.com/firsi/ </em></a><em> </em></strong></p><p><a href="/assets/docs/ancien-site/2024-05-flyer-fr-firsi.pdf"><strong><em>Download the flyer</em></strong></a></p>
