<p><strong>The French Grandes Écoles Scholarship (GEF)</strong> is offered to outstanding Moroccan students, subject to financial need, who have completed the preparatory classes for the Grandes Écoles (CPGE) and been admitted through competitive entrance exams to the first year of the <strong>“Grande École Program” </strong>at one of the eligible engineering schools. </p><p><strong>Scholarship duration:</strong> The Foundation's scholarship is awarded <strong>for three years of study</strong> starting from the 2025-2026 academic year.</p><p>It is suspended in the event of repeating a year, a gap year, a paid internship, an apprenticeship, a professional contract or discontinuation of studies. </p><p><a href="/assets/docs/ancien-site/2024-12-communique-gef-2025-2026.pdf">Read the 2025-2026 GEF Scholarship Programs press release</a></p><p>Application deadline: <strong>May 15, 2025</strong> <br><br><a href="/en/programme-gef/">Apply for the scholarship</a> <br> </p>
