<p>Gilles Spenlehauer, Head of Department of Sciences and Skills of the Future at L’Oréal Group, shares his vision of innovation: how to initiate it and how to turn it into value for the company.</p><p>This event is part of the Conference Series launched by FIRSI, in collaboration with UM6P France, to support its Moroccan scholars throughout their academic journey.</p><p>In his opening remarks, Khalid Baddou, Chief of Staff to the President of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University, recalled the Moroccan university's goals, namely promoting Moroccan talent and research in support of innovation. Its STARS SILVER certification, which recognizes the sustainability of its educational ecosystem, and its long list of VIP visitors are all rewards and encouragement to pursue its mission.</p><p>Gilles Spenlehauer, Head of Department of Sciences and Skills of the Future at L’Oréal Group, then shared with FIRSI scholars his vision of innovation, its close relationship with research and its importance within companies.</p>
