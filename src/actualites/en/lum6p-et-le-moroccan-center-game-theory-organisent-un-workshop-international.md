<p>The Moroccan Center of Game Theory aims to advance research on rational decision-making, with diverse applications across fields such as industrial organization, auctions, voting, blockchains, mechanism design, bargaining, fair division, multi-agent learning and cybersecurity.</p><p>The center hosts an excellent PhD program entitled “Game Theory in Mathematics, Economics and Computer Science,” run in collaboration with universities around the world. </p><p>As part of the “Game Theory Days” program, UM6P and the Moroccan Center of Game Theory are hosting an international workshop on January 22 and 23, 2024 at the UM6P Rabat Campus (ABS Building). It will be an opportunity to present recent work by faculty members and partners. </p><p>The event will be followed by an open day on January 24, 2024, offering an opportunity to explore the benefits of pursuing a PhD in game theory.</p><p>For more information, <strong><em>please contact</em></strong>: <a href="mailto:msgt@um6p.ma">msgt@um6p.ma</a></p>
