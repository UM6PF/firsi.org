<p>On November 15, the <strong>Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation (FIRSI)</strong>, in partnership with <strong>UM6P France</strong>, was delighted to welcome <strong>Claudie Haigneré</strong>, astronaut and passionate advocate for innovation, for an <strong>inspiring</strong> evening at UM6P's Paris premises.</p><p>During this unique event, Ms. Haigneré spoke with passion about her exceptional career, her experiences in space and her vision for the future of science and innovation. </p><p>Part of FIRSI's annual program of events for its scholars in France, the evening gave our young students a rare opportunity to talk with a figure as inspiring as Claudie Haigneré.</p>
