<p>As part of the Inspiring Encounters organized by the Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation (FIRSI), CentraleSupélec was pleased to welcome Mohamed Marfouk, Director of Operations at LVMH, on November 19.</p><p>Originally from Casablanca, this CentraleSupélec alumnus came to share his experience with the foundation's scholars, shedding light on their own aspirations and on the opportunities opened up by their path of excellence.</p>
