<p>On November 25 and 26, the Ibn Rochd Foundation for Sciences and Innovation (FIRSI) was pleased to host a meeting between a delegation from CentraleSupélec, a FIRSI partner, and representatives of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P). It was a valuable opportunity to reaffirm the foundation's ambition to strengthen academic and scientific synergies and collaboration.</p><p>Members of CentraleSupélec's leadership, academic department and foundation toured the research facilities and met with researchers at the university in Benguerir.</p><p>They then met the teaching staff of the Lycée d'Excellence Mohammed VI Polytechnique (LYDEX), an initiative for high-potential African youth from all social and geographic backgrounds.</p>
